# Three-minute showcase script

**Before you start (2 minutes, once):** laptop and phone on the *same* Wi-Fi (or the laptop on the phone's hotspot). `npm run showcase`, open `http://localhost:3000` on the laptop, press **Start a live garden session**. Look at the QR code in the host panel: the address under it must **not** say `localhost`. Put one or two of your own photos on the phone beforehand (JPEG/PNG, not HEIC). Have the laptop's **Sound** off (it is by default) and your screen shared/projected.

**Backup if the network misbehaves:** press *Menu → Leave*, choose **Try the fictional demo** — the same garden, three invented memories, nothing uploaded. Say so plainly: "this is the fictional demo".

| Time | You do | You say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00** | Show the garden from the gate. Press **G** for the whole-garden view, then **G** again. Point at the four signs. | "Singapore, 2040 — a proposed future. This is a shared garden for family photographs. Four areas: Everyday Moments, Celebrations, People We Cherish, Places We Remember. It is empty, because nothing appears until someone chooses to share." |
| **0:20** | Show the big QR (*Show large QR*). Hold up the phone and scan it. | "Anyone in the household scans this with their phone. No app, no account." |
| **0:35** | On the phone: read the notice aloud (*"Your photo and story will be visible to people viewing this session after host approval."*), choose the photo, type a title and a one-line story, pick an area, tick the permission box, **Send for approval**. | "The contributor sees exactly who can see it, and has to agree first. The photo is resized and its location data removed on the server." |
| **1:10** | Laptop: the **Approvals** badge appears. Open the tab. Show the large photo and story. | "Nothing reaches the garden without the host's say-so. I can approve, keep it out, or delete it." |
| **1:25** | Press **Approve and plant**. Watch the plant grow in its area. | "…and it grows live, no refresh." |
| **1:40** | Click **Walk there** in the toast (or click the plant). The memory panel opens with the real photograph and story. | "The real photograph, in its own proportions, with the story in the contributor's words, and the year — or 'date uncertain'." |
| **2:00** | On the phone: open the memory's *Add a recollection* QR (laptop: *Let others add theirs from a phone*), write "I remember it differently…", send. Approve it on the laptop. A leaf appears. | "A relative can add what *they* remember. Both versions stay side by side — neither overwrites the other. Each recollection grows one leaf; no plant is 'more successful' than another." |
| **2:25** | Show the four conversation prompts. | "These are for the dinner table afterwards: *what happened before this photo? Who is missing because they took it?*" |
| **2:35** | Upload (or approve in advance) an everyday memory, a celebration and a place, so the gathering place grows (or use the demo). Press **G**. | "When the garden holds an everyday moment, a celebration and a place, a gathering place grows in the middle. It is cooperative; there are no scores." |
| **2:50** | *Garden* tab → **Delete** on one memory → confirm. The plant vanishes. | "The contributor — or host — can remove it at any time. The photo file is deleted from the server. It cannot unmake a screenshot, and we say so." |
| **3:00** | Finish with *Session* tab: point at the 4-hour expiry and **Delete all session data**. | "It is temporary showcase storage, not long-term preservation." |

## If something goes wrong

- **QR says `localhost`:** host panel → *Change the address phones will use* → click the laptop's address.
- **Phone cannot connect:** try the hotspot; check the firewall prompt was allowed for private networks.
- **Phone says HEIC unsupported:** pick a screenshot or a JPEG; fix later with Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.
- **Plant did not appear:** look at the Connection chip; *Reconnecting…* heals itself when the server is reachable.
- **Start over:** *Session → Delete all session data*, then *Start a live garden session* again.
